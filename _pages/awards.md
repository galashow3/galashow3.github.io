Honors & Awards
======

**Excellence Award — 8th of 985 teams (top 0.8%)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Sep 2026  
*BARAM 2026: 3rd Wind Power Generation Forecasting AI Competition* · Korea East-West Power, GS E&R, Taebaek Gadeoksan Wind Power, DACON · Team leader (3 members)

- Forecast hourly output of three wind-farm groups (17 turbines) over 8,760 hours with 38 LightGBM models built on physics-based features such as a SCADA power curve and air density × wind speed³.
- Added reverse-time validation (train on 2024, validate on 2023) and adopted only changes that improved both directions. The public rank stalled at 59th; the final private rank was 8th.

**Top Excellence Award — 3rd place** &nbsp;·&nbsp; May 2026  
*24th University Student Project Competition* · Korean Society of Industrial and Systems Engineering (KSIE) · Team of 5, data collection and model design  
Real-time anomaly detection for additive manufacturing based on the Laplacian spectrum

- Built a 3σ control chart on λ<sub>2</sub> of the normalized graph Laplacian of 6-axis vibration signals, using only three normal prints as the baseline.
- Detected an internal-pattern defect in 20 s (LSTM autoencoder 63 s, one-class SVM 65 s) and was the only method to catch an internal void defect; implemented as a real-time FastAPI/WebSocket dashboard.
