I am an undergraduate student at Hanyang University, majoring in Industrial Engineering with a double major in Mathematics. As an undergraduate researcher in the Smart Manufacturing and Data Analytics (SMDA) Lab, advised by [Prof. Jihoon Chung](https://cjh7.github.io/), I studied which samples to label first when labels are expensive, using active learning for defect detection on manufacturing surfaces and semiconductor wafer maps.

I am interested in data-driven methods for manufacturing and energy systems: detecting anomalies with few labeled examples, forecasting from sensor and weather data, and solving the optimization problems that arise in production.

Research Interests
======

- **Methodology**: Active learning, anomaly detection, time-series forecasting, combinatorial optimization
- **Application**: Quality assurance in manufacturing (semiconductor wafer maps, additive manufacturing), renewable energy forecasting
